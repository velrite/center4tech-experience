# Teaching approach

All RECOLLECTION unless labelled otherwise. Class of 13 students at the Ogudu center, with one assistant.

## Shape of a session

1. Students presented what they had learned and practised at home.
2. I taught new material.
3. A hands-on lab exercise followed.

## Order of topics

I taught in the order of my own industrial-training report (a private document written after the work, on 31 October 2023, so it is a reconstruction):

1. Technology and how it has advanced
2. Electricity, then electrical versus electronic devices
3. Engineering design concepts
4. Tools, components, the multimeter, resistor colour code, circuit symbols, breadboarding
5. Circuits: simple, series and parallel, pushbuttons, capacitors, potentiometer, transistor, DC motor, 555 timer, water-level indicator
6. Sensors, programming, microcontrollers, Arduino
7. Arduino exercises, then veroboard, then prototype projects

## How I explained things

- I used examples students already knew: a Tesla stopping to avoid an accident, the human body, and daily life. I also researched images to make ideas concrete.
- I found out what each student understood and cared about, then built the explanation from that. One student who hated washing plates became very interested after I explained electronics and robotics through a dishwasher.
- Newcomers kept arriving. I started them with what technology and robotics are, and the old and new ways of doing things, and gave them one-to-one catch-up.
- Faster students got special tasks to keep improving.
- My assistant helped with whatever was needed: teaching newcomers, taking the class while I built, or building while I taught.

## Safety

Students did not use the soldering iron or the hot glue gun because they are dangerous. I did the hot gluing myself. I remember no safety incident.

## Presentations and assessment

- Students presented their work to me and to a classmate. I corrected them and helped build their confidence. I watched them present.
- I assessed students with a checklist. What it checked and who wrote it are not recalled (UNKNOWN).
- At the Tech Expo my center sent two students to represent it. Mine presented the line-following robot. Parents judged.

## Tech & Career Talk

I led it twice for about 40 students from all courses. The content was the opening of my training report: technology and how it has advanced. I do not recall the format beyond that.

## Limits

I have no record of individual student outcomes, so I make no claims about learning results.
