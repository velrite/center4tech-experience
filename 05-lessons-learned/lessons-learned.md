# Lessons learned

These are reflections written in hindsight. They are interpretation, not records.

1. **Measure before guessing.** A multimeter shows what reads and what does not, and where a connection fails, so a fault can be pinpointed quickly.
2. **Faults are not always electrical.** Several of the faults I remember were mechanical or optical: balance, a weak part, shadows, beam alignment.
3. **Start from the learner.** Explaining through a Tesla, the human body or a dishwasher worked better than a fixed explanation.
4. **Keep the dangerous steps with the instructor.** Students did not use the glue gun or soldering iron.
5. **Say what you cannot recover.** Much of this work has no surviving record. Labelling it plainly is better than filling the gaps.
