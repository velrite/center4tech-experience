# Role and responsibilities

This page separates what my offer letter assigned from what I remember doing. The offer letter is private, so the duties below are paraphrased. An assigned duty is not proof that I performed it, so each row carries its own evidence label.

## Assigned duties and what I did

| Assigned duty (paraphrased) | What I did | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Teach the assigned course using the organization's training model | Taught Electronics/Robotics Design & Coding to 13 students. Each session began with students presenting what they had learned, then new teaching, then lab practice. | RECOLLECTION |

| Guide students through building their projects | Instructed and monitored the group line-following robot and the take-home proximity fan. | RECOLLECTION |

The company supplied the main program, and I made sure the students wrote it and helped when they were stuck. | RECOLLECTION |

## Structure at my center

- One lead instructor and one assistant per course. My course had me and one assistant.
- A coordinator for each center spoke with parents and checked that things ran well.
- HR announced to all staff who was lead instructor and who was assistant. Staff across the program numbered over 130 instructors and assistants.
