# Publication boundaries

This is my own working policy, not legal advice. My contract restricts copying Center4Tech's manuals, worksheets and projects, and disclosing confidential information.

| Category | Status |
|---|---|
| My role, dates, skills, what I did, lessons, in my own words | Public |
| Generic concept diagrams | Public |
| Hardware-only photographs of builds | Published, with Center4Tech's written permission |
| Photographs showing students | Private. Not published without permission from Center4Tech and guardians |
| Offer letter, attachment letters, training report | Private. They contain personal and financial details |
| Company code, manuals, worksheets, curriculum, project files | Never published |
| Other people's names | Never published. Roles only |

## What changes after approval

Center4Tech approved hardware-only photographs in writing (a message or email is the record). They are in `04-evidence/photos/hardware/`. Photographs showing students remain private.

## Update protocol

When evidence changes, I update the evidence index first, then the claims that depend on it.
