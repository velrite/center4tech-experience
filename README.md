# Center4Tech — Engineering & Technical Mentorship Experience

## Overview

In 2023, I worked with Center4Tech as a Lead Instructor for the
Young Inventors Summer Camp, teaching Electronics and Robotics.

My responsibilities combined technical instruction, hands-on
engineering, project supervision, troubleshooting, student
mentorship, and final project preparation.

This repository documents my role, engineering contributions,
project experience, and supporting evidence from that engagement.

## Role

**Organization:** Center4Tech  
**Position:** Lead Instructor — Electronics/Robotics  
**Program:** Young Inventors Summer Camp 2023  
**Period:** July 24 – August 25, 2023

## Responsibilities

- Taught Electronics and Robotics concepts.
- Guided students through hands-on engineering projects.
- Assisted with electronics assembly and troubleshooting.
- Guided students through project construction and coding.
- Supervised safe use of electronics and workshop equipment.
- Helped students troubleshoot failed builds.
- Supported students in preparing project presentations.
- Supervised completion of final projects.
- Supported the technical demonstration of completed projects.

## Engineering Areas

- Hardware/software integration
- Prototyping

- Technical education
- Project supervision
